GOOD TO KNOW

BEFORE YOU
TAKE THE SHOT.

A few quick answers for your first visit.

Can I choose how long to play?

Yes. The demo offers 1, 2 or 3 hours. The owner can define the allowed durations in the live booking system.

Do I pay online?

No. This concept uses payment at the venue; online payments are not part of this first version.

Is this a live booking right now?

The form submits a booking request to the booking system. Requests are checked against overlapping bookings, but the selected time is not confirmed until the owner approves it. The owner management dashboard and automatic notifications are still being set up.
